How the numbers are made. RM per gram = spot gold in US dollars per troy ounce × USD/MYR ÷ 31.1034768 grams per ounce. Purity values multiply that by the fineness (0.9999, 0.999, 0.916, 0.75). They are estimated metal values, not retail quotations; jewellers and dealers add premiums, making charges and their own spreads.

For history charts, each gold close is converted at the most recent published USD/MYR rate on or before that day. This page reports market data and headlines. It does not predict prices or recommend buying or selling.
